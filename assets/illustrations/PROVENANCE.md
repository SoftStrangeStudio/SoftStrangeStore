# Storybook artwork

Two decorative illustrations were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-14. They are decorative brand direction, not photographs of Angel's work. The original PNGs had an alpha channel. The site uses 480px WebP derivatives with alpha retained (quality 83, alpha quality 100). No chroma key was used.

## botanical-left.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent botanical corner for a minimalist handmade storybook art shop website. Primary request: one delicate L-shaped watercolor sprig growing from the lower left corner, with sage olive leaves and two small dusty peach five-petal flowers, a few tiny warm butter-yellow buds, thin cocoa pencil stems. Square composition, artwork occupies the left third and bottom third only, upper right and center completely empty and genuinely transparent. Flat scanned hand-painted gouache and fine pencil illustration, warm muted natural pigments, delicate imperfect edges, no heavy outline. Airy, restrained, refined, adult cozy aesthetic. Transparent background with actual alpha channel everywhere outside painted elements, no paper sheet, no checkerboard pattern, no background shadow, no border rectangle, no lettering, no text. Keep all leaves inside canvas with a little margin. Suitable to overlay an off-white photograph frame.

## botanical-right.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: transparent botanical accent for a handmade art storefront. Create a single small airy curved sprig of sage olive leaves, three tiny pale peach blossoms and a few butter yellow buds, with thin cocoa pencil stems. Composition: the sprig arcs gently around the UPPER RIGHT corner of a square canvas, occupies only its top and right outer thirds, leaves point inward softly, bottom left and central area entirely empty. Style: refined scanned gouache and pencil on paper appearance inside painted strokes only, delicate handpainted muted pigments. Cozy minimal storybook mood, not a wreath or bouquet. Genuine transparent background and transparent center, alpha channel, clean natural edges with no colored fringes. No text, no letters, no solid backdrop, no checkerboard, no frame rectangle, no shadow. Keep a small transparent margin around the entire sprig. This will sit beside a cream photograph border.

## Vector and CSS artwork

The small five-petal flower mark is authored SVG. The product double outline and paper frame are CSS UI graphics, keeping their corners crisp and responsive. Text is HTML and the illustrations do not receive pointer events.

## Typography

Fraunces regular headings with DM Sans regular and semibold body copy. Font sources: Google's Fonts API and the official google/fonts repository. Latin subsets are self-hosted WOFF2, preserving copyright metadata. OFL licenses are included in assets/fonts. No runtime font requests leave the site.

Font source request: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500;600&family=Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,400;9..144,500&display=swap
